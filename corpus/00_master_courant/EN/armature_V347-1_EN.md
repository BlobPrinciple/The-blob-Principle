# BOOK I — THE COSMOGONY OF THE BLOB

*Register: narrative. No statement in this book has the status of proof. The references serve provenance.*

### I.1 — The substrate that is not nothing

Before there is a space, before there is a thing, before there is a before, there is a support. This support has no extension, no duration, no direction. It is not born, does not age, does not disappear. It is not the void, because the void is already something that is missing from something; it is that on which a thing can appear. We call it the substrate, and we give it, in the language of the corpus, the name patient zero: that which is there before the first simplex, in dimension zero (V346 p. 1028). In the cosmogony, the substrate is said to be cold: it has nothing to wear out and no thermodynamics is yet defined in it. This sentence is narrative; Book II will verify separately whether a corresponding formal quantity already exists at Ω⁰. It is eternal, not in the sense of an infinite duration, but in the sense of an absence of time: there is in it neither before nor after (V346 p. 128, 547). It contains no structure and, for this exact reason, no asymmetry: everything that will later be able to distinguish a left from a right, a past from a future, a thing from its opposite, will have to appear after it, never within it. One question remains open, and the narrative does not claim to answer it: what makes the substrate set itself in motion? The corpus marked it as a foundational question from the origin (V346 p. 176, Q-2.4). The narrative begins at the first motion, not before.

### I.2 — The first scission and the two twin lineages

The first event is not the appearance of a thing. It is the appearance of an opposition. The substrate splits. But it does not split into two identical copies: it splits into two lineages of opposite polarity. From the same act are born simultaneously a branch and its opposite, a side and its mirror. In the author's intuition, these are two opposite simplices that are born together from the substrate, and with them two twin universes, each of which is the inverse of the other (V346 p. 1028, 1077). The initial pair is exactly neutral: what one carries, the other carries in negative. But the neutrality of the whole, at every scale, is not a consequence of heredity alone: if one lineage divides more often than the other, the sum is no longer zero. Global neutrality remains exact only if the two universes evolve as mirrors of one another. That, precisely, is the condition that the narrative assumes and that the formalism will have to supply. Each of the two universes, taken separately, is asymmetric; orientation is not created, it is shared between two twins. One thing must be said here, because the narrative must remain honest towards what mathematics later established: the direction of the scission, which twin receives which polarity, is not explained by the narrative. It is a choice made once, at the first division, and then transmitted without ever being remade. The corpus reduced it to exactly that: one bit set at the first division, everything else being inherited (V346 p. 1077).

### I.3 — The simplicial carrier and mitosis

The carrier, that is to say the primordial 0-simplex born of the scission, the vertex itself, belongs to the eternity of the substrate. It is not subject to wear. What is inscribed in it is not erased. This must be said precisely, because the word "simplex" is ambiguous: the edges, the triangles, the tetrahedra that the carrier will later construct are made and unmade; it is the carrier, the vertex, that does not die. The carrier possesses a single capacity: to divide. We call this division simplicial mitosis. One point engenders another, and the edge connecting them is the trace of this engendering. Each mitosis is an engraved act: because the carrier is indestructible, the act of its division remains inscribed forever in the genealogy. Mitosis wears nothing out, loses nothing, heats nothing. It adds. And it transmits. Each child receives the lineage of its parent. The polarity born at the first scission thus descends from mitosis to mitosis, without anything in the subsequent dynamics being able to rewrite it, because the subsequent dynamics acts on what is constructed, never on the act of birth (V346 p. 1028, §96.1).

### I.4 — The three times and the two thermodynamics

There is not one time. There are three, and they do not appear together. Time 1 is eternity. It is the time of the substrate and of the carrier. It has no arrow, no loss, no before or after. It is not a time that flows; it is a time that is. Time 2 is direction. It is born with mitosis. As soon as a carrier has divided, there exists a <!-- p. 7 --> parent and a child, and this order cannot be inverted: what has been engraved remains engraved. It is an irreversibility without wear. Nothing is lost, but something accumulates. This is the first thermodynamics of the Blob: a thermodynamics of inscription, carried by the immortality of the substrate (V346 p. 128, V183). Time 3 is dissipation. It appears only with matter. As soon as there exists an inside, something can transform, lose, heat up, come undone. This is irreversibility through wear, the only one we feel. This is the second thermodynamics of the Blob: a thermodynamics of friction, which dissipates (V346 p. 686, 1204). Two irreversibilities of opposite origins, then: the one because nothing is erased, the other because everything wears out. In the corpus, these three times will be found under the technical names t₀, t₁, t₂ (eternity, mitosis, dissipation), and the two thermodynamics under the names type 1 (accumulation) and type 2 (dissipation).

### I.5 — Point, edge, triangle, tetrahedron

Mitosis does not construct everything at once. It makes levels appear, and each level opens a possibility that the previous one did not have. The point is the possibility of existing. Alone, it can do nothing. The edge is the possibility of relation. Two existences become connected. It is the first structure, and it is still reversible: it can be undone without losing anything. The triangle is the possibility of closure. Three mitoses that do not extend into a chain but loop. For the first time, there is a frontier, a possible circulation, a potential inside. The triangle is the weft, that which holds together (V346 p. 178). The tetrahedron is closure become volume. For the first time, an inside really exists. The corpus names these levels Ω⁰, Ω¹, Ω², Ω³, and the order of their appearance is not a convention: each level requires the previous one.

The narrative uses the word "closure" here in the intuitive sense. Book II will strictly distinguish the triangular boundary, the filled 2-simplex and the dynamical rule capable of passing from the one to the other: these three objects are not identical.

### I.6 — The birth of matter

Why matter? Because matter is the only means, for the impetus [élan], of having an inside. Before it, everything is surface, everything is link without interior. The tetrahedron fabricates the first inside, like an egg (V346 p. 1167). And in this inside, everything changes. What is enclosed can be stored, transformed, lost. Time 3 switches on. Dissipation is born exactly here, at the formation of the tetrahedron, neither before (there was nothing to wear out) nor after (matter is constituted). Matter is therefore mortal whereas its carriers are not. It is one of the most singular ideas of the Blob, and it must remain visible: the carrier is eternal, what it constructs is not. Matter is also that which has a proper duration, a rhythm, a breathing. Each structure carries its intrinsic time, and nothing can be imposed on it from outside without deforming it. The corpus ended up making a rule of method of this: one measures the proper time of a system before interrogating it, never the reverse (V346 p. 1907).

The coincidence "first inside = first dissipation" is a founding intuition of the narrative. V347 does not transform it here into a theorem: its status is decided solely in Book II.

### I.7 — The inherited opposition: matter and antimatter

The two lineages issuing from the scission each construct tetrahedra. But they construct them with opposite polarity. On one side what we call matter; on the other its mirror orientation, antimatter. Antimatter is not a defective or rare matter. It is the exact twin. Everything that one of the universes constructs, the other constructs inverted, and the sum of the two remains zero. The twin brings no new information: it is entirely determined by ours, like a reflection (V346 p. 1077, fact iii). In our universe, the dominant lineage is that of matter; in the twin, that of antimatter. But the narrative does not say that our universe contains only matter: it says that it leans. The two lineages coexist everywhere they have been transmitted, in coherent domains that the dynamics does not mix (V346 p. 1028–1029).

### I.8 — The bath

When the two lineages meet, they annihilate. A structure and its mirror undo each other. But annihilation is not sterile. In the author's intuition, it produces the bath: an undifferentiated reserve, without polarity, that feeds all subsequent construction. What comes out of the bath are the products of annihilation, what we shall later call photons; what remains after the bath is the residue, the excess of the dominant <!-- p. 8 --> lineage that annihilation could not pair, because it removes the symmetric and never the asymmetry (V346 p. 1204). The bath is therefore at once the end of one generation and the beginning of the next. It is the place where the universe recycles itself.

### I.9 — Chirality

Here is the place where the narrative must yield before mathematics, and it is better to say so here than to hide it in Book II. The author first thought that the opposition of the two lineages would suffice to produce, in each universe, a net preference for one hand: that viability, by selecting what persists, would also select a chirality. The corpus showed the contrary, and it showed it against the initial desire: viability is blind to parity. A configuration and its mirror image have exactly the same viability. No selection, no viability-driven mitosis can create or erase a hand (V346 p. 1077, 1204).

The narrative survives this correction, but transformed. Orientation does exist, but it is not fabricated by selection: it is inherited from the genealogy, from the lineage born at the first scission, which viability never rewrites. And for this genealogical orientation to effectively couple to what is constructed, something must be posited, a coupling, whose origin the narrative does not supply. Book I says: the opposition exists from the genealogy onward. Book II will say: the viability functional does not transport the sign; a lineage variable and a coupling are necessary, and they are posited.

### I.10 — Everything appears, not everything persists

This is perhaps the deepest principle of the Blob, and the one that gave it its name. The impetus tries everything. It pushes, it overflows, it tries all possible configurations, continuously, without a plan. Most of these attempts lead nowhere: they come undone as soon as they are formed. But some hold. And what decides which ones hold is viability (V346 p. 1167). Viability is not a force. It does not create the attempts and does not constrain them. It is a filter of existence: what has the form of persistence is conserved and reproduced; what does not have it is undone, recycled, transformed. The universe is therefore not constructed because a final plan was inscribed at the beginning, but through three things together: variation, which proposes; viability, which retains; memory, which transmits what has been retained. And this selection has a price. Nothing structures itself for free. For a configuration to hold, order must be paid for in disorder rejected into the surroundings. This is how the arrow of time 3 is born: not as a decree, but as the invoice of every structure that succeeds.

In the narrative, "everything appears" signifies the absence of a final plan and the richness of variation; it does not yet presume that the mathematical state graph is irreducible. Likewise, "to persist" is a cosmogonic concept for which Book II must supply an independent definition.

### I.11 — Construction and continuous victory

The universe is then constructed through a loop that does not stop: scission ⟶ construction ⟶ encounter ⟶ annihilation ⟶ bath ⟶ new construction, under the control of viability at each turn. At each generation, the matter born from the bath is more viable than that which preceded it, because what did not hold has been recycled and what did hold has been transmitted. This is what the author calls the continuous victory of matter: not a victory won once, but a victory replayed at each turn, through residue. There is a hierarchy in this loop. The tetrahedra assemble into larger structures which, in their turn, are subjected to the same sieve: test, keep, transmit. The same mechanism repeats at each level; only the brick and the binder change. And when this construction is looked at from afar, erasing the details, it must resemble itself: the large structures obey the same law as the small ones. This is what the corpus ended up calling G0, the renormalization gate, and it is the place where the program is working today.

### I.12 — The resulting world

Up to here, no word of the real has been necessary. The narrative has not needed Maxwell, Einstein, the Standard Model, Planck or ΛCDM. The reader must be able to stop here, telling themselves: I understand the world the author had in mind, before knowing whether it resembles ours.

The question of whether it resembles it is another question, which belongs to another book. It will be posed in a strict form: for each object constructed in Books I and II, what does it resemble in the observed world? And it will admit only four answers: identity, compatibility, analogy, failure. Nothing of what is found there will be able to come back and modify what has been said here.

### I.13 — The why

The corpus contains a chapter that the author wrote in the first person, and which says what drives the program: the impression of an impetus to mutate, to surpass itself, to occupy all available space; viability as a tipping point; matter as the shelter of the impetus; the living as the moment when viability ceases to be undergone and comes to be sought; consciousness as the organ through which the impetus turns back upon itself (V346 p. 1167–1169). V347 keeps this chapter in its place, in its register, that of openness and not of demonstration. It has no twin in Book II, and it will not have one. It is the only deliberately asymmetric pair of this book.

### II.2 — The lineage variable

Twin of I.2.

Definition 2.1 (genealogy). Let 𝒢 be the directed acyclic graph of the vertices generated by mitosis (defined in II.3), an oriented edge v ⟶ v′ meaning that v′ was born from v. A lineage is a function σ : V(𝒢) ⟶ {−1, +1} such that σ(v′) = σ(v) for every edge v ⟶ v′ (the child inherits from the parent).

Split axiom [A]. At the first division, the two immediate descendants receive opposite lineages: σ₁ = −σ₂. The choice of which of the two receives +1 is a binary variable σ₀ ∈ {−1, +1}, posited, not derived (V346 p. 1077: “exactly one bit posited at the first division, everything else being derived”).

Proposition 2.2 (global neutrality) [T, trivial]. Under the split axiom and heredity, if the initial split is followed by the same number of mitoses in each lineage, Σ_v σ(v) = 0. More generally, the sum is determined by the cardinalities of the two subtrees alone.

Measurement 2.3 [M][ARCH]. On the cascade regime (N = 1500, 3 seeds), the fraction of vertices with σ = +1 is 0.498 / 0.494 / 0.512 ≈ 0.50; the fraction of interface edges (connecting two opposite lineages) is 5–6%. The edge-exchange dynamics does not mix the two lineages, which remain coherent in two domains at ~94% (V346 p. 1028, §96.1).

**Sources.** BLOB PRINCIPLE — CORE 1.0 and its Claim Ledger for the current statuses; Master V346 (1918 pages) as provenance archive and source of the cited pages; V347.0 as the first armature.

**Version rule.**

- V347.0: first mirrored armature.
- V347.1: canonical armature after the third causal audit.
- V347.x: armature and sifting iterations as long as Books I–III are not complete.
- MASTER V347: name reserved for the complete corpus, once Books I and II are frozen and hashed.
- V348: first substantial modification after the freeze of MASTER V347.

**What V347 does not claim.** It derives neither T³ from Ω⁰, nor a physical clock from Gibbs, nor the bath, nor two complete universes, nor a generational improvement, nor a continuum limit, nor an observed physical theory. These absences are ROs, not rhetorical blanks.

***Corde de rappel*** (the author's safety-line principle; literally “abseil rope”). Nothing beyond what the corpus carries. No object invented to save a chapter. Every correction must be able to lower the programme's score. The depth of the Blob is preserved; only the ambiguity between intuition, definition, proof and identification is forbidden.
