# Governance of the laboratory

## Author and scientific direction

**Rudolphe Mirante** is the author, the founder and the sole father of the Blob Principle (viability principle). He provides the scientific direction of the programme and is its final arbiter. In this capacity, he alone decides:

- on the integration of a contribution into the corpus;
- on any change of epistemic status ([T], [R], [FAIL]…);
- on the production and freezing of master versions (V347, V348…);
- on any change to the licences and to this framework.

## Decision principles

1. **Status discipline** — a status changes only on the basis of proof, reproducible measurement or refutation. A status downgrade is a result just as much as a promotion.
2. **Transparency** — supersession decisions are published, with stated reasons.
3. **No new master version without real progress** — a new [T] result or the closing of a gate; never for a negative result or a control campaign.
4. **Documentary autonomy** — each master version is readable by an external reviewer without reference to earlier versions.
5. **Integrity against appropriation** — no exclusive assignment of rights in the scientific corpus (masters, code, data) to a third-party entity, so that no private company can close down or divert the programme.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Author, scientific director | Rudolphe Mirante | Final decisions, freezing of versions, licences |
| Contributors | Any person who has accepted the CLA | Proofs, refutations, replications, critiques, translations |
| Reviewers | Contributors invited by the author | Adversarial review of contributions |

## Recognition

Each integrated contribution is credited **by name** in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) and in the master version that integrates it. The moral right (*droit moral*) of each contributor in their input — in particular the right to be recognised as its author — is guaranteed.
