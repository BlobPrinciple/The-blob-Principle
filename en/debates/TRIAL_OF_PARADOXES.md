# The Trial of the 22 Paradoxes

> **TOE demonstrated: NO** · physical gates 0/7 · level 1 (reproducible mathematical model).

The Blob narrative, in Rudolphe Mirante's three books *Du simplexe au complexe*, gives a place to twenty-two great paradoxes of cosmology, physics and philosophy: why something rather than nothing, the arrow of time, the matter-antimatter asymmetry, dark matter, the measurement problem, consciousness…

**A question for the world: how many of these answers stand up to mathematics?**

The laboratory's AI pilot conducted a first investigation, for and against, comparing each answer in the books with the canonical armature V347.1, which is authoritative. Provisional verdict:

| Class | Meaning | Number |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | answer mathematically established in the armature | **0** |
| **B** | structurally coherent reading, but conjectural | **3** (matter-antimatter, cosmological constant, dark matter) |
| **C** | reframing of the problem, without proof | **14** |
| **D** | not addressed, retracted or corrected | **5** |

No paradox is in class A today. This is not an admission of failure: it is the exact map of what remains to be proven. The Blob gives each great paradox a place in a single narrative — **not yet a solution**. The trial is open, and the verdict can change.

## Your role in the trial

**DEFI-D1 — The juror (level 0, no technical skills required).** Choose a paradox and contest its classification: too harsh or too generous. Give the paradox number, the class you propose, your argument and the page cited (book or armature). Reply in the trial's GitHub issue. Every argued objection gets an answer; if it is accepted, the classification changes here and your name enters the honour roll.

**DEFI-D2 — The advocate (level 2).** Move a paradox up one class. The ladder is fixed:

- **D → C**: propose an explicit Blob mechanism that addresses the paradox, without contradicting the armature;
- **C → B**: give this mechanism a formal object defined in the armature (operator, measure, observable);
- **B → A**: derive from it a demonstrated statement [T] in the canonical sector (layer B), without relying on an archived result [ARCH] or on a historical [T] that has not been recomputed.

A proof that the move up is impossible (and therefore that a paradox must move down) is worth as much as a move up. The three candidates closest to class A are paradoxes no. 5, 8 and 9; the open research problems involved are RO-03, RO-10 and RO-26 (see [PROGRAMME_STATUS.md](../PROGRAMME_STATUS.md)).

**Rules of the trial.** The books remain the reference for the narrative; the V347.1 armature is authoritative for mathematical statements; no historical [T] is admitted without recomputation. Quotations are short and sourced. Arguments are judged, never people.

## Summary

1. As it stands, the thesis "the Blob answers every paradox" is not tenable: none of the 22 paradoxes is in class A, 3 are in B, 14 in C, and 5 are not addressed or have been corrected.
2. The books themselves leave the first movement (A2), the σ₀ bit and the hard problem of consciousness open.
3. Defensible wording: "The Blob Principle proposes a unified reading, through a single mechanism (division, closure, selection by viability), of some twenty great paradoxes of cosmology, physics and philosophy. None is demonstrated to date: three are testable conjectures, most are reformulations, and the questions of origin remain open by decision."
4. The real contribution of *Du simplexe au complexe* is economy: a single grammar (point, link, closure, volume, selection) applied at every scale, with a falsifiable register of 27 open research problems.
5. The established results are mostly negative: mitosis alone does not close (μ-only no-go), viability is blind to parity (orientation must be inherited), and transmission under change of scale (R-TRUE, pull-back) is CARRIED.
6. The uniqueness of the mechanism at every scale is tested precisely at the G0 gate (fixed point C5, universality C7, RO-13 to RO-16; challenges DEFI-G1 to DEFI-G4). That is where the thesis is decided, not in the list of paradoxes.
