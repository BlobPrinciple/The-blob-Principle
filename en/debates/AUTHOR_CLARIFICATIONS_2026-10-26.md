## 1. The six clarifications

**P1 — Dissipation is constructive first.** [I] The binders calcify, the structure stiffens, and that very stiffening is what makes it fragmentable. Fragmentation then yields mixed groups — tetrahedra, triangles, edges, vertices — and not an ordered descent from one dimension to the next. The author explicitly corrected the ordered reading that had been put to him.

**P2 — Regeneration.** [I] Renewing the binders before rupture extends a structure's viability. This is the most immediately testable of the six: the laboratory's engine already knows how to compare two paired populations, with and without renewal of links. Opens **DEFI-M17**.

**P3 — Viability is a filter that acts before matter.** [I] It sorts the possibles explored in t₁; only viable configurations pass into the time of matter and dissipation, t₂. Viability would then not be a property observed after the fact on what exists: it would be the condition of entry. Touches RO-20 (defining "persist" without circularity), RO-21 (accessibility) and RO-23 (dissipative threshold). Opens **DEFI-M19**.

**P4 — The information → trial → meaning loop.** [I] "Knowing is not understanding": information becomes meaning only once a structure has undergone it, and the enriched information is kept in the Whole, not in the structure that underwent it. The armature today has no quantity representing this loop. It is a proposition of the narrative, out of reach of measurement as things stand; the laboratory says so rather than dressing it up as a result.

**P5 — One and the same mechanics at every scale, at different speeds.** [I] The speed depends on how far the binders have calcified. This is the strongest form of the Blob's scale invariance: from the tardigrade to the whale, from the living to the galaxy cluster, a single mechanism and different clocks. It is also the most exposed — see part 2. Touches DEFI-G4 (universality across scales). Opens **DEFI-M18**.

**P6 — The substrate differentiates itself "in spite of itself".** [I] Asked whether the substrate *seeks* to know itself — which would presuppose an intention — the author answers "in spite of itself": it is the act of differentiating that produces knowledge, not an intention aiming at it. The consequence is as practical as it is philosophical: this version can be formalised, the other cannot.

## 2. The Kleiber tension: what the laboratory already measures

This point does not come from the dialogue. It comes from confronting P5 with results the laboratory has already published, and it is the main reason these clarifications are recorded here.

**The living.** Kleiber's law (1932): metabolism follows B ∝ M^(3/4). The West–Brown–Enquist model (1997): internal times follow M^(1/4). These are **power laws**. An animal fifteen times heavier is not a hundred thousand times slower: it is about twice as slow.

**The Blob's engine.** Laboratory advances no. 9, 10 and 12 (DEFI-M12, DEFI-M13, DEFI-M14), on abstract graphs of mean degree 4, under π ∝ exp(n·S_sim/τ):

- the sojourn in the aristocracy follows log t½ = 0.964·√n − 1.77 from n = 17 to 193, and the prediction still holds at n = 225;
- at n = 257 the law is exceeded by 30 to 41 %, measured by two engines with no shared code;
- the aristocracy is a dominant clique of size ≈ n^0.47.

**An exponential in √n is not a power law.** From n = 17 to n = 257 the measured slowdown is of order 10⁵. If one identified n with mass — and that is precisely what would have to be written down before any comparison — Kleiber would give, for the same size ratio, a factor close to 2. Five orders of magnitude separate the two forms. If "the same mechanics at every scale" holds in the sense of P5, the living should show the engine's form. It does not.

**Three honest ways out; only one can be measured right now.**

1. **The measured regime is not the one of the living.** DEFI-M12 established that the slowness is carried by the cooling λ = n/τ of axiom A4, and **that at fixed λ it disappears**. An organism is not a system whose temperature decreases with size. At fixed λ the law could become a power law again — and that is exactly what **DEFI-M18** asks.
2. **The correspondence between n and mass is ill-posed.** Then it must be written down explicitly, and defended, before speaking of scales.
3. **The Blob's scale invariance, in the sense of P5, is false.** That would be a refutation, and here it would count as much as a proof.

**A trap to avoid.** n^0.47 and M^(3/4) are not comparable: they bear on different objects, the size of a clique on one side, a metabolic power on the other. No numerological pairing of these exponents will be accepted without a correspondence written down and defended beforehand.

The dialogue of 26 October contains four departures from the canonical corpus. They must be corrected in any text drawn from it.

1. **The numbering of the times.** The dialogue sometimes says "time 3" for dissipation, sometimes "time 2". The book and the armature write t₀, t₁, t₂. One numbering, everywhere.
2. **t₁ is not an "irreversible succession".** The armature V347.1 proves the opposite: mitosis is reversible (theorem 3.4). Only the genealogical inscription is irreversible.
