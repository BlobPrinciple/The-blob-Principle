# Code of conduct

The Blob Principle laboratory is a place of scientific work open to all.

1. **Criticise ideas, never people.** The harshest refutation is welcome; contempt is not.
2. **Argue with evidence.** A calculation, a counterexample, a reference, a precise page.
3. **Epistemic honesty.** Do not present an analogy as an identity, a measurement as a theorem, or a hypothesis as a result.
4. **Respect for beginners.** Questions from the public are legitimate; they are answered with patience.
5. **Attribution.** Cite your sources and do not appropriate the work of others.

Messages that breach these rules may be hidden, and their authors excluded from the repository, at the discretion of the programme's author.
