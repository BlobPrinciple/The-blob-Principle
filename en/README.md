## What the Blob Principle is

The Blob Principle is a **humble candidate for a theory of everything and a complete cosmogony**. Its central idea fits in one sentence: *everything appears, not everything persists — and what decides what persists is viability.*

The programme starts from the smallest possible object — a point, the 0-simplex — and seeks to rebuild, level by level, the edge, the triangle, the tetrahedron, then space, time, matter and its dissipation, from a **viability functional** defined on finite simplicial complexes. It does so in two languages that mirror each other:

- **the narrative** (the cosmogony: substrate, scission, twin lineages, mitosis, three times, matter, bath, viability);
- **the mathematics** (the only objects actually constructed, with their proof status).

The gap between the two is not hidden: it is **named, numbered and put to work** in the form of **27 open research problems**. It is to these that this laboratory invites you.

## Why a public laboratory

The Blob Principle was developed alone, independently, since the beginning of 2026: more than **219 versions** of the master document, a current master of **1,918 pages** (V346), hundreds of scripts and numerical campaigns, and three books. This work has reached the point where a single author is no longer enough: it needs **outside eyes, independent replications, proofs and refutations**.

This repository therefore opens the programme to everyone — researchers, students, engineers, the curious — with a single compass: **to carry these research problems through and open a new scientific path**. The ambition is for the Blob Principle to become a TOE candidate developed in an open laboratory, **carried by both the public and scientists**.
