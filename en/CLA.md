# Blob Principle Contribution Agreement (CLA) — version 1.0

This agreement is entered into between **any person who submits a contribution** to the "The Blob Principle" repository (hereinafter "the Contributor") and **Rudolphe Mirante**, author of the Blob Principle (hereinafter "the Author").

## 1. Purpose

The Blob Principle is an original creation of the Author. So that the programme remains coherent, open and protected against any appropriation, the Author must be able to integrate, publish and develop contributions within the corpus. This agreement governs that integration while guaranteeing the Contributor recognition of their work.

## 2. Definitions

- **Contribution**: any text, proof, calculation, code, data, figure, critique or translation submitted by the Contributor on the repository (*issue*, *pull request*, discussion or any other means), with a view to its integration into the programme.
- **Corpus**: all works of the Blob Principle, present and future, including the master versions, books, code and data.

## 3. Moral right of the Contributor

The Contributor retains their **moral right** (*droit moral*) in their Contribution. The Author undertakes to **credit them by name** (name or chosen pseudonym) in the `CONTRIBUTEURS.md` file (English version: [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md)) and in any master version integrating the Contribution, together with a description of their input.

## 4. Licence granted to the Author

The Contributor grants the Author, **free of charge, on a non-exclusive and irrevocable basis, worldwide and for the entire term of protection** of copyright (*droits d'auteur*), the following economic rights (*droits patrimoniaux*) in their Contribution:

1. **reproduction** on any medium, whether known or unknown to date;
2. **representation** (*représentation*) and communication to the public, by any means;
3. **adaptation, modification, correction, translation** and **integration** into the Corpus or into any work of the Author;
4. **distribution** under the licences of the repository, present or future;
5. **sub-licensing** of these rights, for the purposes of publishing and disseminating the Corpus.

This licence is non-exclusive: the Contributor remains free to use their own Contribution elsewhere, subject to article 6.

## 5. Undertakings of the Author

The Author undertakes:

1. to keep the scientific Corpus (master versions, code, data) **freely accessible** to the public for non-commercial use;
2. **not to assign exclusive rights** in the scientific Corpus **to any third-party entity**;
3. to publish, with stated reasons, any supersession decision concerning the Contribution.

## 6. Undertakings of the Contributor

The Contributor warrants:

1. that they are the author of the Contribution, or that they hold the rights necessary to submit it;
2. that the Contribution is not subject to a licence or an obligation incompatible with this agreement (in particular towards an employer or a funder);
3. that they will not present their Contribution, nor any work derived from the Corpus, as a distinct theory claiming authorship of the Blob Principle or of the viability principle (*principe de viabilité*).

## 7. No remuneration and no warranty

The Contribution is provided free of charge and "as is". This agreement creates no relationship of subordination (*lien de subordination*), association or partnership.

## 8. Governing law

This agreement is governed by French law.

## 9. Acceptance

The Contributor accepts this agreement by ticking, in the description of their *pull request*, the box:

> ☑ I have read and accept the Blob Principle contribution agreement (CLA v1.0).

or by writing this sentence in the thread of their contribution.
