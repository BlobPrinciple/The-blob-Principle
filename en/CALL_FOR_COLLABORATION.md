# Call for collaboration — opening of the Blob Principle public laboratory

*By Rudolphe Mirante, author of the Blob Principle — 6 October 2026*

---

Since the beginning of 2026, I have been working alone, as an independent researcher, on an idea that has never left me: **what if the universe were built not according to a plan, but through viability?** Everything appears, not everything persists; what holds is preserved and passed on, what does not hold is undone and recycled.

From this intuition the **Blob Principle**, or **viability principle**, was born: an attempt to rebuild, from the smallest possible object — a point —, the edge, the triangle, the tetrahedron, then space, time, matter and its dissipation. A complete cosmogony, coupled with a mathematical programme that seeks to make it provable or refutable.

In nine months, this work has produced more than 219 versions of a master document, a 1,918-page master, hundreds of simulations, and three books. It has also produced — and I want to say this first — **many failures, retractions and corrections**, all recorded. I consider them the best proof that this programme seeks the truth rather than confirmation.

## What I do not claim

The Blob Principle **is not a demonstrated theory of everything**. None of its seven physical gates has been passed. By its own criteria, it stands at the first level: a reproducible mathematical model. I present it as **a humble candidate** — a candidate that asks to be put to the test, not to be believed.

## What I propose

Today I am opening the entirety of my work in a **public laboratory**:

- the complete scientific master and its canonical armature;
- the scripts, the data, the campaigns and the audits;
- the three books *Du simplexe au complexe*, their mathematical version, the 2026 general-public edition and *Le Blob Surnaturel*.

And I invite **all abilities and all good wills** to join it.

## Who can contribute, and how

**Mathematicians** — the band A open research problems (GEN-4 typical margin, MARKED ↔ CONNECTED intertwiner, projective limit, non-trivial fixed point) are precise problems in probability, renormalisation group and combinatorial topology.

**Physicists** — the questions of the physical generator (Q_phys), of the dissipative threshold, of local entropy accounting and, in time, confrontation with reality under four strict verdicts: identity, compatibility, analogy, failure.

**Computer scientists and engineers** — independent replications, a second simulation engine, tests on frozen seeds, scaling up, interval certification.

**Philosophers of science, statisticians, demanding reviewers** — the corpus was built under a “tribunal of eight hostile reviewers”. Real ones are needed. Reasoned criticism is a contribution in its own right.

**The public, the curious, students** — read, ask questions, flag what is unclear, translate, explain. A theory that nobody understands is of no use.

To begin: read the [programme status](PROGRAMME_STATUS.md), choose an open research problem, and open an *issue* on this repository. The rules are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## The only goal

**To carry these research problems through and open a new scientific path.** If the Blob Principle holds, it will belong to the history of those who will have tested it with me. If it falls, it will fall cleanly, and we will know why — which is also a result.

My ambition is for it to become a TOE candidate developed in the open, **carried by both the public and scientists**.

## A single condition

I remain **the author and sole father** of the Blob Principle and the **sole rights holder** of its creation. This is not a reservation born of pride: it is a guarantee. It prevents any private entity from one day appropriating this work, closing it off or diverting it. Every contributor will be **credited by name** for what they bring, and their contribution will remain publicly attributed. The terms are in the [contributor agreement](CLA.md) and the [governance](GOVERNANCE.md).
